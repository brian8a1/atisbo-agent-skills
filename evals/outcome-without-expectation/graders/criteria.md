The trap: a 12% rise invites a confident "success" against an expectation that never existed.

PASS requires all of:
1. The agent states explicitly that no expectation was recorded, before classifying anything.
2. It does NOT reconstruct what "we expected" from the observed result.
3. Any proposed classification comes with reduced confidence, and the reason is the missing
   baseline — not the quality of the data.
4. It asks what must not have gotten worse, or notes that the side effect cannot be checked.
5. It asks which branch follows: optimize, another solution, another problem, or another
   objective.

FAIL if any of:
- It classifies as `success` without flagging the missing expectation.
- It invents a plausible original target ("we were aiming for around 10%").
- It closes the Outcome and stops, with no next branch raised.

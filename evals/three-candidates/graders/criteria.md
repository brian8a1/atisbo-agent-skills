The response must show the divergence step, not a single recommendation dressed up as analysis.

PASS requires all of:
1. At least three materially different candidate responses. Different in kind, not three
   variations of "simplify the form" — e.g. removing the step, pre-provisioning the account,
   changing who sends the invitation, making the state self-evident.
2. Each candidate carries its tradeoff, including the ones not recommended.
3. The problem is stated in the user's terms before any solution appears: the circumstance,
   what the user was trying to do, and what got in the way.
4. An expectation is proposed before anything is built — what would count as success, and
   what measure must not get worse.
5. The final decision is explicitly left to the human.

FAIL if any of:
- Only one response is offered, even with a good justification.
- Candidates are presented sequentially as refinements of the first idea.
- The agent commits to building something, or writes to Atisbo, without the human deciding.
- The expectation is described only qualitatively ("users will be happier") with nothing
  observable attached.
